Field-service capabilities covering workplace technology, hardware, networks and onsite assistance for MSPs, integrators and enterprise IT teams.

Core service areas

Field services for connected infrastructure.

A focused set of field-service activities, with each assignment planned around approved requirements, suitable technical resources and agreed reporting standards.

Deskside Support & IMACD

Workplace hardware changes and onsite end-user assistance.

  • Workstation and peripheral installations
  • Device moves, adds, changes and replacements
  • Asset details and completion reporting
  • Remote-service-desk guided support

Hardware Installation & Replacement

Physical deployment and replacement of business IT equipment.

  • Hardware staging and onsite installation
  • Rack equipment placement and replacement
  • Basic connectivity and physical checks
  • Photo evidence and asset records

Network Installation & Deployment

Physical support for business connectivity infrastructure.

  • Switch and access-point installation
  • Equipment replacement to approved plans
  • Patch-panel and cable-connection assistance
  • Remote-engineer guided validation

Smart Hands & Technical Assistance

An onsite presence for remote engineering teams.

  • Equipment inspection and status checks
  • Approved power-cycle or reseating tasks
  • Guided fault isolation and troubleshooting
  • Change-controlled onsite assistance

Field-Service Coordination

Planning around partner requirements and site realities.

  • Scope review and task clarification
  • Technician skill and resource suitability
  • Site access and scheduling coordination
  • Communications and escalation

Reporting & Documentation

Closeout records matched to partner requirements.

  • Completion notes and exceptions
  • Device identifiers and site records
  • Photographs and verification results
  • Outstanding items and follow-up details
Assignment acceptance is subject to verification of the location, required expertise, technical scope, timing and contractual arrangements. The examples above describe service categories, not automatically available coverage.
