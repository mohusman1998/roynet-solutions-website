About Roynet Solutions

Technical coordination.
Clear expectations.

A partner-focused IT field-service business shaped by practical coordination experience and a commitment to careful communication and responsible project delivery.

Our story

One brand. An international perspective.

Roynet Solutions originated in Hungary, where the business coordinated onsite IT assignments for international service providers. Roynet is now based in Edmonton, Alberta, with Canada as its primary growth market.

The company's identity is built around understanding technical requirements, coordinating onsite activities and keeping partner teams informed.

We welcome enquiries involving Canada and the European Union, with delivery scope and availability confirmed individually.

What guides us

Practical principles for onsite work.

Understand first

Get clarity on the request, site conditions, technical instructions and what success means to the partner.

Communicate clearly

Agree the reporting process and escalation points so onsite teams and remote stakeholders are aligned.

Close out properly

Define the evidence, notes and outstanding issues necessary to support handover and follow-up.

Future direction

A long-term infrastructure vision.

Roynet's focus is onsite field-service partnerships and infrastructure delivery opportunities. Over time, the company intends to expand its technical delivery model and evaluate complementary capabilities.

Managed IT, cloud administration and cybersecurity remain future business directions, not services currently marketed as operational.

Our focus today

IT field services & coordination

Deskside support, hardware, networking, smart hands, planning and reporting — matched to the scope and confirmed delivery resources of each engagement.
